Born in 1963 in Nantong, Jiangsu Province, Chen Xiangdong is a distinguished member of both the Chinese Artists Association and the Society of American Graphic Artists (SAGA). He serves as the Vice President of the American Chinese Painting Society and was previously the Vice President of the Qidong Printmaking Institute. Since 1997, Chen has lived in New York, where he has continued to develop his artistic career. His work has been featured in numerous solo exhibitions, such as the Metropolitan Art Museum in New York and the Jingtong Art Center in Shanghai. Chen’s participation in major exhibitions worldwide highlights his influence in both traditional Chinese and contemporary ceramic art, bridging Eastern and Western art cultures.
