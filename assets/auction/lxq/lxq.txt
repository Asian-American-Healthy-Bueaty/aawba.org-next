Renowned designer, curator, artist, and patron of art.

In 1985, he participated in the New Wave of Chinese Art Activities, namely the 1985 Art New Wave. In the mid-1990s, he established an international exhibition company dedicated to cultural exchanges mainly with developed countries.

Later, he established 261 International Art Center and many other art parks. He curated several international art exhibitions and events, with a commitment to promoting the development of Chinese and Western contemporary art.


知名設計師、策展人、藝術家、藝術贊助人。自1985年參與中國美術新潮活動，即“八五美術新潮”。九十年代初期起，與藝術界、設計界建立合作；九十年代中期，創辦國際型展覽公司，致力於與發達國家進行文化與經貿方面的交流及展示。後創辦藝術園區、藝術中心，多次策劃、舉辦國際藝術展覽、活動，致力於促進中西方文化的交流。
