譚嘉陵 (Catherine Tan Chan)
 
Dr. Catherine Tan Chan (譚嘉陵) received her Ph.D. in Biochemistry from University of California, Berkeley in 1973, and was granted two U.S. patterns for the development of Prolactin and solid phrase beta-HCG RIA assays.
 
Dr. Chan is the Founder and President of the Foundation for Chinese Performing Arts (www.ChinesePerformingArts.net), a non-profit organization since 1989 for the promotion of Asian heritage through music and performing arts. Currently, over 164 classical concerts have presented world renowned Asian musicians to critical acclaim at prestigious venues such as Boston Symphony Hall, New England Conservatory’s Jordan Hall, Harvard Sanders Theatre, Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum Calderwood Hall, and Carnegie Hall in New York. The Fou Ts’ong International Concerto Competition and Summer Free Concert Series at NEC (since 2019), the Summer Music Festival at Walnut Hill (1990-2018), the All-American Chinese Painting and Calligraphy Competition (since 1991), the Youth Poetry in Silk and Bamboo Chinese Music Instrument Audition and Concert (2008-2017) are highly successful.  Currently she is on the board of New England Association of Chinese Professionals and Chinese American Fine Arts Society. She is a resident member of the Harvard Musical Association since 2014. Dr. Chan was awarded the “Certificate of Recognition 1991” by Boston Mayor Raymond Flynn, the “Official Resolution 2000” by President James M. Kelly of Boston City Council, US Phi Lambda Boston Chapter in 2005, and Chinese Culture Connection in 2005.
 
Dr. Chan has been practicing traditional Chinese brush paintings since 1962.  Her artworks were shown in solo and group exhibitions. She was invited for demonstrations and workshops at the Museum of Fine Arts Boston in 2011, 2014, and 2016; and has been teaching Chinese brush painting at Newton Chinese Language School since 1981.    
譚嘉陵1966年台灣大學農化系畢業，1973年加州大學柏克萊生化博士。繼續在波士頓大學醫學院，從事四年心臟病高血壓及血管硬化方面的博士後研究。繼在藥廠做了四年賀爾蒙RIA微量測定法的研究發展。獲有兩項有關 hCG及Prolactin 測定方法的專利權。
 
1962 年開始學習國晝，師承 邵幼軒、朱念慈、梁丹丰、孫聖嘉、江昭美等大師。擔任麻州牛頓中文學校國畫教師己45年 (1981 年開始)。1989年創辦中華表演藝術基金會 (Foundation for Chinese Performing Arts, www.ChinesePerformingArts.net )。每年定期舉辦各種古典音樂會，傅聰國際協奏曲比賽, 夏日系列音樂會, 全美中文學校國畫書法比賽, 胡桃山音樂營，及絲竹春吟青少年中國器樂比賽等活動。曾任紐英崙中華專業人員協會會長及董事長，美華協會紐英崙分會會長，大波士頓中華文化協會斡事，胡桃山藝術高中 (Walnut Hill School for the Arts) 校董等。現任紐英崙藝術學會，紐英崙中華專業人員協會董事等義工。也是哈佛音樂協會 (Harvard Musical Association) 會員。
